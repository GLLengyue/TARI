## Arrival

At dusk, the old city gate opens for the first time in twelve years. A
blue lantern burns above the arch, and someone inside is waiting for a
name that is not yours. Warden Ilyan lowers his spear but does not step
aside.

## The Archive

Beneath the archive, the register opens to a page scraped nearly blank.
The archivist waits beside it with a candle cupped in both hands. One
line remains: a name, and beneath it an oath written in a different
ink.

## The Oath

Below the register, a stone niche waits beneath the first bell. The
visitor must decide whether the recovered name belongs to the city or to
the person who carried it through the dark.
